### N2 · RoboArena(2506.18123v2)episode 口径:**实为三口径(600 / 612 / 4284),不是两口径**

- **冲突**:摘要 ">600 成对真机评测" vs 正文 "4284 累计 episodes";本轮实抓发现**正文另给精确值 612**,
  即摘要的 ">600" 是 **612 的取整表述**,而 4284 是**另一个量**。
- **外部原始来源**(逐字引句):
  - `https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18123`(HTTP **200**,**47,837** B)摘要:
    > "We instantiate our approach across a network of evaluators at **seven academic institutions** using the
    > **DROID robot platform**. Through **more than 600 pairwise real-robot evaluation episodes across seven
    > generalist policies**, we demonstrate that our crowd-sourced approach can more accurately rank the
    > performance of existing generalist policies than conventional, centralized evaluation approaches…"
  - `https://arxiv.org/html/2506.18123`(HTTP **200**,**343,748** B;v2;236 math / 236 alttext)§1 Introduction:
    > "Through decentralized evaluations of **7 generalist policies across 7 universities and a total of 612
    > pairwise comparisons**, we demonstrate that RoboArena provides more accurate performance rankings…
    > when compared to an "oracle" policy ranking computed via exhaustive evaluation of all policies on all
    > tested tasks. … our work also provides the most comprehensive evaluations of generalist robot policies to
    > date (**4284 evaluation episodes** across numerous tasks and scenes)…"
  - 同页 §5.1 "Comparison"(4284 的**生成机制**):
    > "We establish an "oracle" policy ranking by exhaustively evaluating all policies on all tested tasks and
    > comparing their average progress scores. This was done by **asking evaluators to score the remaining five
    > policies after each A/B evaluation on the same task. In total, we use 4284 evaluations to compute this oracle
    > ranking.**"
  - 同页 §5 正文与 Figure 5 图注:
    > "In total, our RoboArena evaluation covers **4284 policy rollouts** across dozens of scenes and hundreds of
    > task instructions. … To our knowledge, this is the most extensive evaluation of generalist policies to date."
    > "Figure 5: … Right: "Oracle" policy ranking, aggregated from progress scores of **4284 evaluation rollouts**."
- **定案(这是判断,基于上述原文 + 算术闭合)**:
